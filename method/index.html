How the Desk Works

Every note carries a complete research stack: thesis, supporting evidence, valuation framework, named invalidation triggers, position-sizing guidance, and stop level. Subscriber coverage refreshes weekly. The published track record — open to any allocator before they sign — refreshes quarterly. When a thesis misses, the process enforces an erratum within ten business days of the invalidation event, naming the root cause. There are no silent corrections.
